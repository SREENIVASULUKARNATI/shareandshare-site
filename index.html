Share&Share

Share&Share by Setpopkar.

Share&Share is a personal app for capturing and organizing what matters to you — notes, to-dos, alarms, links, files, calculations, folders and calendar items — with EmSree, a voice assistant. Your data stays on your device, and you can optionally sync it to your own cloud storage, such as Google Drive.
